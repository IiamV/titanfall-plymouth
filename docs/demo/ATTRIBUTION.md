# Demo imagery

The black and white GIFs and the visual tracks of their MP4s use the packaged **Maldives SDDM greeter screenshot**, installed at `/usr/share/sddm/themes/maldives/maldives.jpg`.

- Theme: Maldives, by Abdurrahman AVCI, from the [SDDM project](https://github.com/sddm/sddm/tree/develop/data/themes/maldives). Its installed theme metadata lists **CC-BY-SA**.
- Wallpaper credits are recorded in the [upstream theme README](https://github.com/sddm/sddm/blob/develop/data/themes/maldives/README). It credits Dan Grady and identifies the waterbungalow photograph by **Dr. Peter Schmidt**, [source image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nalaguraidhoo_Wasserbungalows.JPG), under [CC-BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- Adaptations: resized/cropped to fill the demo, cut into animated cockpit panels, internal seam animation, and playback at the 1.25× default speed.

The existing preview audio is retained and time-scaled to match. Original reference video, audio source files, and logo remain excluded from the repository.

The separate wallpaper demo uses the original vector illustration in [wallpaper.svg](wallpaper.svg).
